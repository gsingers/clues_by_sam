## Heuristics for a new player (this board)
1. When a clue references "directly right/left/above/below" of a corner/edge set, first delete members where the relation is undefined — it often turns the count into "all remaining".
2. Harvest every number in a compound clue; embedded counts ("the 6 criminals neighboring X") become groups you'll need later.
3. "At least N per column" + a group sitting inside that column is often the only thing that forces the far cell.
4. On an Easy board, the most recent clue almost always resolves the next cell — read it first.
