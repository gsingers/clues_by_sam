## Heuristics for a new player
- When a clue creates an "exactly one of two cells" pair, don't wait to resolve it — plug it into every later count/parity clue as a guaranteed 1.
- "Only 1 of the N criminals neighboring X is Y's neighbor": compute N(X)∩N(Y) first; it's usually 2-3 cells and yields an immediate pair or placement, and then the N total finishes the rest.
- Parity clues become placements the moment all but one cell is known or paired.
